### ENGLISH VERSION

#### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile game **PERFECT PARCEL** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") for Android devices.  
We respect your privacy. PERFECT PARCEL is designed as an **Offline-First single-player game**. The game does not require user accounts, registration, login, or personal profile creation.

#### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We **do not** collect your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- We **do not** access your device contacts, photo gallery, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.
- We **do not** operate private tracking servers or backend player databases.

#### 3. Information Handled Locally on Your Device
All game progression data (level scores, stars, unlocked upgrades, coins balance, daily login streak, and audio/language settings) is stored **exclusively locally on your device** in a private, encrypted/checksum-protected JSON file. This data never leaves your device unless you manually back up your phone.

#### 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob & Google Play Billing)
The Application integrates trusted third-party services provided by **Google LLC**:

1. **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP):**
   - We display advertisements (voluntary Rewarded Video and non-intrusive Interstitial ads) to support game development.
   - For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom (UK), Google UMP presents a consent form allowing you to choose between personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with GDPR.
   - Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and coarse diagnostic data.
   - For more information, please review [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

2. **Google Play In-App Billing:**
   - In-app purchases of coin packs are processed directly and securely through the Google Play Billing Library. We do not store or process your credit card, bank details, or billing address. All financial transactions are managed entirely by Google LLC.

#### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)
PERFECT PARCEL is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through the Application, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

#### 6. User Rights & Data Deletion
Since we do not store your data on external servers, you have full control over your local data:
- You can reset all game progress and statistics at any time via the in-game **Settings ➔ Reset All Progress** button.
- Uninstalling the Application from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes all local save files.

#### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

#### 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
